Three lakes and what they mean for the plumbing.

Upper Prior Lake covers 354 acres and flows north into Lower Prior Lake, which covers 810 acres, through a navigable channel under Eagle Creek Avenue. Spring Lake, at 290 acres, lies partly in the city and sits higher, draining north into Upper Prior Lake. Homes on the shoreline sit close to a high water table.

Close to the lakes, basements stay damp and sump pumps work harder. A sump that fails in a lakeside basement can fill fast. We cover sump pump repair and battery backup, and we find out whether dampness is groundwater or a pipe before suggesting either.

Prior Lake's water: iron removal and hardness.

The City of Prior Lake operates seven production wells drawing from the Jordan and the Franconia-Ironton-Galesville aquifers, with a treatment facility that removes iron and manganese. Its public page lists total hardness at 19.3 grains per gallon, which is very hard. The city also notes pH of 7.2.

That hardness scales water heaters and fixtures quickly. A water heater repair call in Prior Lake often traces to scale on a lower element. A softener sized to 19 grains makes a difference, and our softener page explains the options.

Wells, septic and city water.

Not every Prior Lake property is on the city system. The city's public pages point owners of private wells and septic systems to the Well Owner's Handbook and a guide to septic systems. We service plumbing inside the home for both. Septic tank pumping and well pump work are separate trades, and we say so if a job belongs with one.

If you are on city water, the Shakopee Mdewakanton Sioux Community also shares part of the system under a cooperative agreement. That affects supply, not the pipes inside your house.

Prior Lake calls by season.

Spring brings wet basements and failing sump pumps. Summer brings seasonal homes opening up with water heaters that sat idle. Fall is when hose bibs need winterizing. Winter brings frozen lines in unheated lake cabins and garages, where a burst pipe can flood a space no one is using.
