Fall: winterizing and water heater check.

Before the first freeze, disconnect hoses, shut the interior valve to outdoor bibs and drain irrigation lines. Check the water heater's anode rod and drain valve before it works harder. Lakeville's water is treated with chlorine and potassium permanganate to remove iron and manganese, according to the city, and sediment still builds in a tank over time.

Winter: frozen lines and boiler calls.

Winter calls are frozen pipes and heating trouble. A burst pipe in an exterior wall or garage can flood a finished space. Hydronic and radiant homes may need boiler repair. If a pipe has frozen but not burst, open cabinet doors, raise the heat and call us.
