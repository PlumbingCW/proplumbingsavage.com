An overflowing toilet is not a wait-until-Monday problem. We answer 24/7 in Savage for toilet emergencies and offer same-day visits when we can. Close the shutoff valve behind the bowl by turning it clockwise, lift the tank lid and push the flapper down if water is still coming.

If water is already on the floor, towels and a wet vac buy you time. Keep power strips and extension cords out of the wet area.

When a toilet problem needs help now.

  • Water rising in the bowl after a flush.
  • Water seeping from the base onto the floor.
  • A tank that cracks or leaks at the bolts.
  • A toilet that will not stop running and runs up the water bill.
  • Gurgling in the tub when you flush, which suggests a drain issue.

A single toilet that clogs may only need a plunger or an auger. Several fixtures acting up together points to the main line.

Repair or replace?

Replacing a flapper or fill valve is a small job. Replacing a cracked bowl or an inefficient old model can be a better use of money than repeated repairs. If the toilet is more than twenty years old and wastes water, a new unit often pays back over time.

We bring common parts on the truck and can source a replacement toilet if you have not picked one. If you have chosen your own, we install it.

Savage homes and toilet habits.

Hard water leaves mineral deposits that clog the small holes under the rim. Savage buys most of its water from Burnsville, which reports 25 to 26 grains per gallon. A rim clean can bring back a strong flush.

Wipes labeled flushable cause real trouble in Savage lines and downstream. Keep them out of the bowl. If a toilet clogs repeatedly, the cause may not be the toilet at all, and we check the drain beyond it.

Small checks that save a service call.

Lift the tank lid and look. If the water level sits well below the fill line, the toilet will flush weakly. If water trickles over the overflow tube, the fill valve is leaking through. Dropping a few drops of food coloring in the tank and waiting twenty minutes without flushing shows a leaking flapper if color appears in the bowl.

These checks take five minutes and tell us a lot when you call. They also keep you from replacing the wrong part.

If the toilet rocks, tighten the closet bolts gently, just until it stops moving. Overtightening cracks porcelain, which turns a small fix into a replacement.
