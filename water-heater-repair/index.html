Cold showers at six in the morning, a pilot that blows out or a puddle at the base of the tank all mean the same thing: the water heater needs a plumber now. The line is open 24/7 for these calls, and same-day visits are often available in Savage.

Before we arrive, check the breaker or gas control, look at the tank for drips and note whether the hot water runs out fast or never gets warm. If water is spilling, close the cold supply valve on top of the tank and switch off power or gas.

When a water heater problem is an emergency.

A tank that leaks steadily, a smell of gas near the unit or a hot, hissing relief valve is urgent. Leave the area if you smell gas, call the gas utility or 911 from outside, then call us after it is safe. A cold shower alone is annoying but not dangerous.

Many hot water calls from other markets start with a leak. A weeping fitting or relief valve is repairable. A seam leak in the tank is not, and we will say so on the spot rather than sell you a part that cannot fix it.

Many tanks fail because the anode rod was never replaced. If yours is a few years old and still sound, a new rod can add years. If it is past a decade, we explain when a repair simply postpones a replacement.

Why Savage water heaters scale up.

The water Savage drinks comes mostly from Burnsville's system, where hardness is reported at 25 to 26 grains per gallon. That is a lot of dissolved calcium and magnesium. It settles on the tank floor and coats the elements, which makes them work harder and burn out sooner.

Flushing a young tank once a year slows that buildup. A softener upstream helps more. We can discuss a water softener if scale is eating your elements.

Cost and decision factors.

Most repairs are modest, and a replacement is a different order of cost. We give a price for the repair and say honestly if the tank is past saving.

The failed part.An igniter, an element and a gas valve are priced differently.
Tank age.Spending real money on a tank near the end of its life rarely pays off.

Access.Cramped closets and corroded fittings slow the work.
Time of day.After-hours repairs are priced above weekday ones.

Some water heaters carry a manufacturer's parts warranty. Look for the date on the data plate and the original paperwork.
