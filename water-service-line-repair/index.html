The water service line runs from the main in the street, through the meter, to your home. When it leaks or breaks, you may see a wet or sunken patch in the yard, lose pressure or watch your meter run. We answer 24/7 in Savage for service line trouble, and same-day visits are often possible.

If water is flowing heavily, close the main valve inside the house. If the leak is before the meter, ask your utility to shut off at the street.

When a service line leak is an emergency.

  • Water bubbling in the yard or driveway.
  • No water or very low pressure at every tap.
  • A meter that moves with all fixtures off.
  • Muddy water after a sudden pressure drop.
  • Ground that is soft, sunken or green in one strip during winter.

What your utility and your plumber each handle.

A water service line crosses a boundary. The utility owns the main in the street and usually the tap that connects to it. The homeowner usually owns the pipe from there to the house. The meter and curb stop sit near that boundary. Because ownership and shutoff authority differ, a leak before the meter can require a call to the utility, while a leak after it is yours to repair.

We help you work out which side the leak is on and we coordinate shutoff and inspection with the utility. Ask City of Savage Utility Services about your address before work begins so nothing is done twice.

If the line is old, replacing the whole private run is often cheaper than repairing the same pipe again next year. We show you both quotes.
