A main drain that backs up again and again is a candidate for hydro jetting. We run 24/7 for backups, and same-day visits are available when we can fit them in. Stop running water, keep the washer off and clear the floor around any drain that is gurgling.

Jetting uses a high-pressure hose with a nozzle that spins water against the pipe wall. It scours grease, scale and root hairs that a cable only pokes through. It does not fix a cracked or collapsed pipe, and that is why we look first.

When a drain backup calls for jetting.

  • A main line that clogs every few months after being cabled.
  • Slow drains throughout the house, not just one fixture.
  • A kitchen line or restaurant drain coated with grease.
  • Tree roots that grow back within weeks of cutting.
  • Floor drains that burp or smell when the washer drains.

If sewage is entering the basement, treat it as urgent and call at once. Keep people out of the wet area until it is cleaned.

Camera first, then jet.

We prefer to put a camera in the line before we use pressure. That shows a sag, a broken joint or a collapsed section where high pressure could make things worse. If the pipe is sound, we jet. If it is not, we explain the repair options.

Savage details that matter.

Hard water in Savage, drawn largely from Burnsville's supply at a reported 25 to 26 grains per gallon, leaves mineral scale that narrows pipes over time. Jetting is one of the few ways to remove it. Older clay or cast iron lines under the older parts of town, including the blocks around downtown along Highway 13, collect roots and scale.

Always call Gopher State One Call at 811 before any digging. Minnesota's service is free and asks for at least 48 hours notice, not counting weekends and holidays. We bring this up if jetting reveals a repair that needs excavation.

What jetting cannot do.

Jetting cleans a pipe that is still a pipe. It cannot rebuild a crushed section or reconnect an offset joint. If the camera shows a defect, we say so and stop. A clean, honest answer beats a pressure wash that hides a failing line for a few weeks.
