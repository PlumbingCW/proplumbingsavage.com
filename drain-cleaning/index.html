A drain that holds water, gurgles or backs up into the tub is a problem that gets worse by the hour. We run 24/7 for clogged drains in Savage, and same-day appointments are often available. If a floor drain is overflowing, stop running water in the house and leave the washer and dishwasher off.

For a single clogged sink, a bucket and a few minutes of patience are enough to wait for us. For several fixtures at once, the clog is likely in the main line and every flush adds to the mess.

Drain backup: what to do first.

  • Stop using water. Every flush or load of laundry sends more down a blocked line.
  • Check whether one fixture or many are slow. Many slow fixtures point to the main drain.
  • Keep chemical drain openers out of the pipe. They can splash, and they leave caustic water for the plumber to work around.
  • Move rugs and stored boxes away from basement floor drains.
  • Call the 24/7 line and describe which drains are affected.

Sewage that rises in a basement floor drain is a health issue. Keep children and pets out, and open a window if the smell is strong.

Why Savage drains clog.

Savage's water arrives hard, mostly through Burnsville's supply, and Burnsville reports 25 to 26 grains per gallon. Hard water leaves a mineral film inside pipes that catches grease, soap and hair faster than soft water does. Many homes here also have laundry and kitchen lines that run to a single main under the slab or basement floor.

Homes near mature trees, including the older blocks around the Hamilton District and downtown along Highway 13, are more likely to have roots in clay or cast iron service lines. Newer plastic drains in the subdivisions off County Road 42 tend to clog from grease, wipes and flushed items rather than roots.

Snaking and cabling a drain.

A drain cable is a rotating steel line that bores through a clog or hooks it so it can be pulled out. It is the right tool for a soft blockage in a sink, tub or branch line. It is a poor tool for scale, heavy grease or roots that regrow, because it opens a hole through the mess rather than cleaning the pipe wall.

If a drain was cabled and clogged again in a few weeks, the pipe wall is probably still coated. That is the point where hydro jetting or a camera look makes more sense than another pass with the cable.

Kitchen sink drain clogs.

Kitchen drains clog differently from bathroom drains. Grease cools inside the pipe and traps food, and a disposal can push a lot of that debris down in one go. The clog often sits at the trap or in the horizontal run under the floor.

Stop running water into a sink that is backing up, and do not mix chemical cleaners, because they can burn skin and damage pipes. If a disposal is involved, see our garbage disposal repair page. Otherwise call and we explain what we would check first.

Keep drains clear between visits.

Wipes marked flushable still do not break down. Grease belongs in a can, not the sink. A hair catcher in the tub drain costs a few dollars and prevents the most common bathroom clog. If a drain slows again within a few weeks, ask us to look with a camera before you buy another bottle of cleaner.

For bigger jobs we can move up to sewer line cleaning, and for root-choked lines we often recommend hydro jetting. Neighbors in Prior Lake and across the river call us for the same reasons.
