Sewage in the basement, gurgling toilets and a smell near a floor drain mean the sewer line may be broken, offset or full of roots. We take these calls 24/7 in Savage and offer same-day visits when we can. Stop using water, keep people out of any wet area and call us.

Even if a drain cleaning clears the line, the same problem can return if the pipe itself is damaged. Our job is to find out, show you the evidence and give you honest repair choices.

Sewer backup: what to do first.

  • Stop using sinks, toilets, tubs and the washer.
  • Keep children and pets away from standing sewage.
  • Turn off power to a flooded basement only if you can reach the panel safely.
  • Take photos for your insurer.
  • Call the 24/7 line.

If you can, find your cleanout. Do not open it while sewage is under pressure. We handle that safely.

How we confirm the problem.

A camera inspection is the starting point. The scope shows cracks, offset joints, sagging sections, roots and collapsed pipe. We record the footage and explain what we see, so you understand the repair before you agree to it.

Questions to ask before you approve a dig.

  • Can I see the camera footage of the failure and the location mark?
  • Is lining possible, or does the pipe shape rule it out?
  • What will the yard, driveway or sidewalk look like afterward, and who restores it?
  • Does the quote include a final camera pass to prove the new section is sound?
  • What warranty covers the work, and what does it exclude?

A repair quote that cannot answer these is a quote you should pause on. We put the answers in writing with the price so you can compare.

Cracked pipe and spot repairs.

A crack in a sewer line, often in PVC, cast iron or clay, lets roots and soil in. A camera pass shows where the crack sits and how far the damage runs. If the rest of the line is sound, a spot repair can fix just that section instead of replacing the whole run.

Whether a spot repair holds depends on the pipe material, the depth and how much of the line is damaged. We show you the camera footage and explain the options, including when a full replacement is the honest answer.
