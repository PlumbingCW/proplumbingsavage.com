A water heater that is leaking from the base, rumbling like a kettle or sending out rusty water has usually reached the end of its life. We answer the line 24/7 for exactly this call, and replacement in Savage is often possible the same day when a matching unit is on the truck or at a local supplier.

While you wait, find the cold water shutoff valve on the pipe above the tank and turn it clockwise until it stops. If the heater is electric, switch off its breaker. If it is gas, turn the gas control knob to the off or pilot position. Then put a bucket or a wet vac under any drip.

When a water heater failure is an emergency.

A slow weep at a fitting can wait for a morning appointment. A steady stream, a tank that is hot to the touch and hissing, or water spreading toward a finished basement cannot. Water on the floor near a gas unit or an electrical panel is the one case where you should stay clear and call us from another room.

  • Water pooling around the base of the tank, not just damp pipes above it.
  • Rusty or brown hot water that clears when you run only the cold tap.
  • A tank older than about a decade that has started popping, banging or whistling.
  • No hot water and a pilot or igniter that has been replaced once already.

After hours, we handle the shutoff walk-through by phone first. That keeps the damage from growing while a plumber drives out, and it lets us bring the right size and fuel type the first time.

Repair or replace: how we help you decide.

A new thermocouple or heating element is cheap next to a new tank. A tank that has rusted through is not repairable at any price. We check the age on the data plate, the condition of the anode rod, the shutoff valves and the venting before we recommend anything.

Hard water and Savage tanks.

The City of Savage buys about 71.5 percent of its distributed water from Burnsville, and Burnsville's own water report puts its hardness at 25 to 26 grains per gallon. Savage's report does not list a hardness figure, so test your tap or ask City of Savage Utility Services what your address receives.

Hard water leaves scale on the bottom of a tank and around the heating elements. That scale is why many local tanks get noisy and lose capacity before the steel itself gives out. A water softener upstream of the heater cuts that buildup, and we mention it only when the numbers support it.

What decides the price.

Replacement cost swings with the unit you choose and the work around it. We quote the whole job before work starts, so you see one number.

Tank size and efficiency.A bigger or higher-efficiency tank costs more up front and may cost less to run.
Fuel type.Gas, electric and heat pump units use different parts and different venting.
Venting and gas line.Moving a vent or adding a sediment trap changes labor.
Code and access.Tight closets, stairs and old shutoff valves add time.
Extras.Expansion tank, drain pan, new valves or a recirculation line are separate line items.

Home insurance often covers sudden water damage from a failed tank but rarely the tank itself. Ask your carrier before you throw out soaked drywall.
