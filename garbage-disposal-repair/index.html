A disposal that hums but does not spin, leaks under the sink or has stopped altogether can wreck a dinner and flood a cabinet. We answer 24/7 in Savage for disposal emergencies, with same-day service when we can. Turn the switch off, do not put a hand in the drain and, if it is leaking, shut the water to the sink.

When a disposal problem is urgent.

  • Water leaking from the bottom of the unit.
  • A burning smell or smoke.
  • A sink full of standing water that will not drain.
  • A humming motor that will not turn.
  • A tripped breaker every time you run it.

Always turn off the disposal at its switch and the breaker if needed before reaching in. A hand in a disposal is never safe.

What Savage kitchens throw at disposals.

Hard water scale builds on the grinding chamber and drain, particularly with Savage's supply drawn largely from Burnsville at a reported 25 to 26 grains per gallon. Cold water running during use and for a few seconds after pushes grease and scraps down the line. Avoid pouring grease down any drain.

Disposals handle small food scraps, not coffee grounds in bulk, potato peels, bones or fibrous vegetable waste. When someone ignores the guidance, a jam or a clog follows.

What never to put down a disposal.

Grease and oil harden in the trap. Fibrous stalks such as celery and corn husks wrap around the grinding parts. Coffee grounds pack into a paste. Bones, fruit pits and glass can jam or chip the blades. Pasta and rice swell and clog the line. Run cold water before, during and for about fifteen seconds after use to carry food through the trap.

A disposal that smells can be cleaned with ice and a little citrus peel. A disposal that smells because food is rotting in the drain line needs a plumber.
