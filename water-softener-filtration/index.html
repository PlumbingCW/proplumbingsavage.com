A Savage note on radium.

The City of Savage has posted a radium update. It reported a 2023 test where radium at Water Treatment Plant 3 was above the limit, took that plant offline and states the situation is not an emergency. The City's page says water softening, reverse osmosis and distillation may help reduce radium, and that carbon filters are not recommended because radium can build up in them.

That is a reason to ask your installer how a unit performs, not a reason to panic. Contact City of Savage Utility Services with health questions and check with a doctor if you are concerned.

Salt, space and upkeep.

A salt-based softener needs a spot near the main water line, a drain, an electrical outlet and room to carry bags of salt. Plan the location during the water test. The brine tank should stay above a minimum salt level, and the salt should be loose, not packed in a crust.

Resin lasts for years, but it can foul with iron. An iron cleaner added to the brine tank on a schedule helps. Filters need new cartridges on the maker's schedule, and a neglected one can make the water worse, not better.

We set hardness and regeneration to fit your actual usage, not a default. Over-regenerating wastes salt and water. Under-regenerating lets hard water through. Ask us to recheck the setting once a year.
