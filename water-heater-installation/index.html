Size the tank to the household.

Capacity is not a guess. We count bathrooms, adults, teens and whether a soaking tub fills at the same time as the dishwasher runs. A two-person household and a family of five need different tanks, and a bigger tank than you need costs more to heat every day.

Typical sizing guide.
Household.Common tank range.Notes.
One to two people.30 to 40 gallons.Single bath, light use.
Three to four people.40 to 50 gallons.Most common family size.
Five or more, or a large tub.50 to 75 gallons, or tankless.Peak demand drives the choice.

These ranges are common starting points, not promises. Recovery rate and first-hour rating matter as much as the tank size printed on the label.

Fuel, venting and location.

Gas units need a vent path that meets the manufacturer's rules and a gas shutoff within reach. Electric units need the right breaker and wire gauge. Heat pump (hybrid) models need room for air flow and a drain for condensate. We check each before we quote.

Savage basements are often finished or partly finished. A drain pan with a pipe to a floor drain or sump is a small add-on that prevents a big loss if the tank ever fails.

Savage-specific points.

Hardness in the supply Savage buys from Burnsville is reported at 25 to 26 grains per gallon. A new tank will scale up sooner than you expect without a softener, so we raise the topic during the site check. See our water softener and filtration page for what that adds.

Newer builds near the Highway 13 corridor often have a utility room with floor drain access. Older homes in the Hamilton District can have tight closets or shutoffs that have not turned in decades, and we price the extra work honestly.

Questions to settle before the install day.

Decide where the old unit's drain will run, whether you want a recirculation loop for faster hot water at distant fixtures, and what temperature you want it set to. A setting near 120 degrees balances scald risk and comfort for most households. Tell us if someone in the home is very young or elderly and we adjust the plan.
