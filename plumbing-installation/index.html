A bathroom remodel, a basement bath, a new kitchen island sink or an addition all start with the same question: where do the supply, drain and vent lines go? We install new plumbing for Savage homeowners and builders, and we help plan the layout before drywall closes the walls.

What we install.

  • Bathroom and kitchen fixtures, from faucets to showers and tubs.
  • Rough-in for remodels, basement baths and additions.
  • Laundry hookups and utility sinks.
  • Outdoor hose bibs and frost-proof sillcocks.
  • Water treatment hookups, ice maker and appliance lines.

If you have not picked fixtures yet, call before you buy. Rough-in dimensions depend on the model, and a shower valve that does not match the trim wastes money.

Savage-specific points.

Minnesota's plumbing code is Minnesota Rules chapter 4714, administered by the state Department of Labor and Industry. Anyone who contracts for plumbing in a community of more than 5,000 people needs a plumbing contractor license, and the department offers a free Check a License tool. Use it before you hire anyone.

Savage's water arrives hard, with Burnsville's supply reported at 25 to 26 grains per gallon. New fixtures with fine aerators and cartridges scale up quickly. Planning a softener during an addition costs less than adding one later.

Remodel timing: when the plumber should come.

Plumbing comes in two visits on most remodels. The rough-in happens when the walls are open and before insulation and drywall. The finish happens after tile, cabinets and paint are done. Missing the rough-in window means cutting finished walls later. Tell us your general contractor's schedule and we line up the visits.

Bring us in early on bathroom layouts. A shower moved eighteen inches can change where the drain runs and where the vent must go. Small design choices save large amounts of plumbing work.

Order fixtures before the rough-in visit. Shower valves, tub spouts and wall-hung faucets each have specific rough-in dimensions, and guessing wastes time.

Fixtures we are often asked to install.

Pedestal and vanity sinks, undermount kitchen sinks, pot fillers, island sinks, utility tubs, freestanding tubs, shower systems with multiple heads, bidet seats and outdoor frost-proof hose bibs come up regularly. Each has a rough-in and a supply need. A pot filler needs a cold line to the wall behind the stove. A freestanding tub needs a floor-mounted or wall-mounted filler and a drain location that fits its footprint.
