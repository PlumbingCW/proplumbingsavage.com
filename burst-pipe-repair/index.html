Why pipes burst in Minnesota.

Water expands when it freezes. A plug of ice in a pipe builds pressure between the ice and a closed faucet, and the pipe splits where it is weakest. The burst often happens when the ice thaws and water starts to flow again.

Repair and what comes next.

Once the water is off, we locate the break, cut out the damaged section and replace it with new pipe and fittings. We then pressure-test the system. If the pipe was frozen, we check other exposed lines for damage.

Drywall, insulation and flooring may need drying or replacement. We stop the water and fix the pipe. A restoration company handles drying and mold. Take photos before anything is moved, for your insurer.

Savage winter notes.

Savage winters bring deep freezes, especially in unheated garages, crawl spaces and exterior kitchen walls. If you travel in winter, keep the heat on, open cabinet doors on cold nights and ask a neighbor to check the home. Pipes along the north and west walls are at the most risk.

If you plan any digging near a water line, call Gopher State One Call at 811. It is free and requires at least 48 hours notice, not counting weekends and holidays.

After the repair: protect the house.

Once the pipe is fixed, run a fan or dehumidifier in the affected area and keep the heat on. Wet insulation and drywall can grow mold within a couple of days. If water reached finished surfaces, call a restoration company the same day.

Document everything with photos before cleanup, including the broken pipe. Keep our written repair description for your insurer.

Cracked or split pipes.

Not every pipe failure is a dramatic burst. A hairline crack or a split along the seam can leak slowly for weeks. Freeze damage is a common cause in Minnesota winters, and the split often sits where a pipe runs through an unheated wall or crawl space.

Signs include a damp spot, a drop in pressure, a hissing sound or a bill that climbs. Shut off the water to that line, open a faucet to relieve pressure and call. We cut out the damaged section and replace it, and we check nearby pipes that saw the same cold.
