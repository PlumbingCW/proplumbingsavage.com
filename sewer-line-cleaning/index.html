What a main line cleaning does.

A drum machine sends a cable with a cutting head down the line from the cleanout. It cuts roots, breaks up paper and grease and pulls debris back out. For heavier buildup, we may follow with a hydro jet, and we can run a camera to confirm the result.

Why Savage mains clog.

Savage's water is hard, drawn from the Burnsville system at a reported 25 to 26 grains per gallon. Scale gradually narrows pipes, and the same scale holds grease. Mature trees near older lines around the Hamilton District, and along streets toward the Credit River, put roots into joints.

Newer homes along the Highway 13 and County Road 42 corridors may have shorter laterals and plastic pipe. Their clogs usually come from the inside, from grease, wipes and food.

Is a cleaning enough?

If a cleaning gives you a year or more of free-flowing drains, it was the right fix. If the same clog returns within weeks, the pipe likely has a defect. We show you the footage so you can decide with facts.

Two or three clogs in a year is a signal. At that point a sewer inspection is cheaper than another emergency call.

Floor drains, cleanouts and what to leave alone.

Most basements have a floor drain near the water heater or laundry, and many have a capped cleanout nearby. Keep both accessible. Do not store boxes over them, and do not pour paint, solvents or cooking oil down either one. A floor drain connects straight to the same main that is backing up.

After a cleaning, pour a pitcher of water into each floor drain to refill the trap. A dry trap lets sewer gas rise into the house, and the smell is often mistaken for a new clog.

We label the cleanout location in your service notes when we leave, so the next visit, ours or anyone else's, starts faster.
